Nexora Privacy Policy 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora ("we", "us", or "our") may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Nexora Discord bot and related services. 2. Information We May Collect Depending on how Nexora is configured and used, we may process: Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs. Moderation-related information, such as message IDs or moderation actions. Bot settings and configuration information for Discord servers. Basic technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Nexora should only collect information that is reasonably necessary for its features and operation. 3. How We Use Information Information may be used to: Provide and operate Nexora's features. Perform moderation and server-management functions requested by authorized users. Maintain server configuration and preferences. Detect abuse, security problems, or misuse of the service. Fix bugs and improve reliability. 4. Message and Content Data Nexora may process Discord content when a feature requires it, such as moderation, reporting, filtering, or other server-management functions. Nexora should not retain message content longer than reasonably necessary for the feature that uses it. Server administrators are responsible for configuring Nexora in a way that complies with their own rules and applicable laws. 5. Data Storage and Retention We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Some configuration or moderation records may remain until they are deleted, automatically expire, or are no longer required. 6. Sharing of Information We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or infrastructure providers when necessary to operate Nexora, or when required by law. Nexora also uses Discord's platform and APIs. Discord's own policies and privacy practices may apply to information processed by Discord. 7. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Nexora. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. 8. Your Choices and Data Requests If you believe Nexora has stored information associated with you and you want to ask about deletion or access, contact the Nexora administrator through the official support method provided with the bot. Server administrators may also remove Nexora from their server, which may stop Nexora from processing new information from that server. 9. Children's Privacy Nexora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe information has been collected improperly, contact the Nexora administrator so the matter can be reviewed. 10. Third-Party Services Nexora may rely on third-party services, including Discord and hosting or infrastructure providers. These services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. 11. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when Nexora's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when the policy is updated. 12. Contact For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Nexora administrator through the official support/contact method associated with the bot. 13. Important Note This Privacy Policy is a general template for Nexora and should accurately reflect how your bot actually collects, uses, and stores data. If Nexora uses databases, logs, analytics, external APIs, or stores message content, update this page to describe those practices accurately.
